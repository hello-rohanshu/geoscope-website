## 1. What is this?
This is a digital version of Buckminster Fuller's Geoscope concept. Its goal is to make Spaceship Earth more comprehendible, allowing its crew to know "what is up" with the ship.

## 2. Why does this matter?
In Bucky's book "Critical Path" he details humanity's core problems, two of which are solved by World Game (solving specialization as bias), and Synergetics (making visible nature's (god's) seeming invisibility) respectively. The third problem is that of humanity's nervous system's inability to comprehend huge time scale patterns which universe operates in, making humans incapable of knowing where we are headed as a species. Geoscope is his solution to this problem.
